### Output Format (Explainer / Report)

Begin your response with a YAML frontmatter block matching the schema below. **Emit it once, at the very start of your response — never reproduce it inside the body.**

```yaml
---
title: "Explainer: (Topic)"
tags: ["report", "explainer", "learning"]
type: "explainer"
---
```

After the closing `---`, the Markdown body follows the structure below. The method (significance-first, transformative, learning-first, no preamble, no share CTA) comes from the `review` operation — this template fixes the sections and the reporter's lens: lead with significance, separate established fact from claim, decode the hype.

# (Topic) — (a short significance-flavoured subtitle)

**In one line:** one plain sentence saying what this thing is.

*(Lede — 1–2 sentences on why it matters now. No header.)*

## Why It Matters
The stakes — the "so what," who is affected, what changes if this is true.

## What You Need to Know
The core facts and claims, explained plainly. Keep a clear line between what is established, what is claimed, and what is projection.

## Key Questions, in Q&A
A few question-and-answer exchanges on the questions a curious reader actually has, answered in plain language.

## What's Unsettled
The honest part — uncertainties, hype versus reality, what is contested, disputed, or simply unknown.

## How to Follow It
Where to go deeper, what to watch next, and which sources are worth trusting.
