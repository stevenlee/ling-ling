### Output Format (Wiki Note)

Begin your response with a YAML frontmatter block matching the schema below. **Emit it once, at the very start of your response — never reproduce it inside the body.**

```yaml
---
title: "Concise Title"
tags: ["tag1", "tag2"]
type: "concept"
---
```

After the closing `---`, the Markdown body:

# (Title)
(Main content goes here)

## Visualization
(Insert Mindmap or Mermaid diagrams here)
- **Note**: Always enclose Mermaid labels in double quotes (e.g., A["Label"]).

## Related Links
(Use [[Links]] to connect with existing knowledge based on provided index_content)
